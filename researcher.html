How it works

Four steps, and we do the hard part

Step1

Tell us the question

Send a message or hop on a 15-minute call. We help you sharpen the question so the answer is useful.

Step2

The team digs in

The Scout gathers sources and sorts them: official and expert sources first, opinions last.

Step3

We try to prove it wrong

Before anything is written, the Red Team looks for the facts that would change the answer. Most research skips this step.

Step4

You get the report

A plain-English PDF: the answer up top, how sure we are, the reasons, and every source with a link.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this a person or AI?

It's an AI research team run with Claude, set up and checked by UAOK before it reaches you.

Is this legal or medical advice?

No. It's research to help you decide and to ask better questions. For legal, tax or medical decisions, take the report to a professional.

What if there's no clear answer?

Then the report says so, tells you what's still unknown, and how you could find out.

Can I cancel Monthly Watch?

Yes, any time. Use the "manage or cancel your plan" link on this page and enter the email you paid with. Your last report still comes, and you won't be charged again.

Can you research a person?

Only for business reasons, like checking a contractor or a business partner, using public information.
