Questions

Before you buy

Do I need to know anything about tech?

No. Claude explains every word before using it and waits for you to say "done" before the next step. If you get stuck, it can look at your browser with you and point to the right button.

What do I need before I start?

A Mac, Claude Code on a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher), Google Chrome, and about 45 minutes for the first setup.

Do I have to show my face?

No. You can use a made-up AI person, a cartoon character, or go fully faceless with voiceover videos, text slides and hands-only pictures. Claude asks which one you want when you start a project.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. It's Mac only for now. A Windows version is coming.

Will it post for me?

Only if you want it to. By default your posts and captions are saved in your Output folder for you to upload. With the optional auto-send service, they arrive as drafts you approve.

What's the CRM, and does it cost extra?

It's your private notebook for the business side: brand deals, what you're owed, due dates, contacts and your Instagram and Facebook DMs. It's included in every plan. Free on your Mac, or about $5/month for hosting if you want it online on your phone. Reading DMs needs the optional auto-send service.

Can I run more than one brand?

Yes. Each brand or persona is its own saved project, with its own photos, profile, voice and output folder.

How real does my voice sound?

The Professional clone, made from about 60 minutes of reading, sounds the most like you. The Quick clone is close, but not perfect. You hear a sample video before you pay for a full batch.
