About Our Church

Rooted in Christ. Reaching for more.

Ultimate Advancement of Knowledge Church is a Baptist congregation where the Gospel is central and every person is family.

Our Story

A church for the whole person.

We are a Christian, Baptist church that holds Scripture as our foundation and the love of Jesus as our calling. We gather to worship God, to grow in the knowledge of His Word, and to serve the world He loves.

Our name is our mission — the ultimate advancement of knowledge begins with the fear of the Lord and blossoms into wisdom, character, and purpose. We make disciples, strengthen families, and lift up our communities.

Whether you join us in person or online, you belong here.

What we believe

  • The Gospel of Jesus Christ. Salvation by grace through faith.
  • The authority of Scripture. The Bible as our guide for life and faith.
  • Baptist tradition. Believer's baptism and the priesthood of every believer.
  • Knowledge & discipleship. Growing minds and hearts together.
  • Love in action. Serving our neighbors and community.
