What Is an E-Invitation?

More than a card. A home for your event.

A paper card gets read once. An EPS web invitation is a personal website for your event, designed to match your theme. Share one link by text, email or social media. Guests see the date, place and dress code, RSVP in seconds, and leave video and photo messages. You get a private dashboard that keeps the guest list for you.

How It Works

From idea to invitation in four steps.

Tell us about your event

Send us a request with the occasion, the date, about how many guests, and your theme or colors. A photo or artwork you love helps too.

We design it

We build your invitation from a ready-made theme or a custom look made to match your photo, and choose the features you want.

Review & share

You see a preview, ask for changes, and then get your link plus a printable flier with a QR code to share anywhere.

Guests RSVP — you relax

Watch RSVPs and shout-outs come in on your private dashboard. After the event, the page turns into a thank-you page with a slideshow.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. Your invitation is a website. Guests just tap the link on any phone, tablet or computer — nothing to install and no account to create.

How do I share it?

You get one link to send by text, email, group chat or social media, plus a printable flier with a QR code for anyone who prefers paper.

Can I change details after it's live?

Yes. From your private dashboard you can update the date, time, place and notes, swap the top photo, upload an invitation video, and show or hide sections — no tech skills needed.

Who can see my guest list?

Only you. Your dashboard is password-protected. Guests see only the invitation and the shout-outs, never other guests' contact details.

Can I use my own photo or colors?

Absolutely. Choose one of our ready-made themes, or send us a photo or artwork and we'll design a custom look with matching colors and fonts.

How far ahead should I book?

The earlier the better, so guests have time to RSVP. Reach out as soon as you have a date in mind and we'll work with your timeline.

How much does it cost?

Every event is different, so pricing depends on the features you choose. Send us your event details and we'll put together a quote.
