Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan a QR code and it opens in their phone's browser.

Do I need my own website?

No. Full Pro comes with one. If you already have one, the Party App links from it.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Ask for the free demo and we'll make one with your name on it.

What do I need at the party?

Your phone. Add an iPad for the photo booth and a TV or projector for the big screen if you want.
