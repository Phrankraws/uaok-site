Questions

Before you buy

Do my clients need an app?

No. They use the texting, Instagram or Facebook they already have.

What if a client wants to cancel?

They reply CANCEL. The time opens back up on your calendar.

Can I cancel Monthly care?

Yes, any time. Use the "manage or cancel your plan" link on this page and enter the email you paid with. Care runs to the end of the month you already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Can I still book people myself?

Yes. Anything you add to your calendar is blocked out for the bot.

What if someone texts something else?

The message comes to you like normal, so you can answer it yourself.
