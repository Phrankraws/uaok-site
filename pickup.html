About the App

Built for the way crews actually work.

Airline schedules change fast. Pick Up puts control back in your hands — swap, give away, and pick up shifts in seconds, keep an accurate record of every hour and every dollar, and stay in touch with your crew, all from one app.

Why Crews Love It

Benefits for airline employees.

Trade shifts in seconds

Fly with your buddy

How do I trade or pick up a shift?

Open Type of Trades from the home screen and choose Buddy Bid, Shift Trade, or Giveaway. Post the shift you want to move or browse what's available, then confirm the trade with a tap.

How does Opus track my hours and pay?

In Opus, enter your start and end times, any absence type, your pay period, and pay rate. The app calculates your regular and overtime hours so you have an accurate record of what you worked and earned.

Is my information private?

Your account and schedule information are used only to operate the app's features. We do not sell your personal data. For any privacy question or request, email info@uaok.org.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email info@uaok.org from the address on your account and request deletion. We'll remove your account and associated data.

Which devices does Pick Up support?

Pick Up is built for iPhone and Android phones. Availability on the App Store and Google Play is coming soon — check back here for the download links.

We'd love to hear it. Email info@uaok.org with a short description (and a screenshot if it helps) and we'll follow up.
