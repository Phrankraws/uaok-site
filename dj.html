What's inside

Everything the party needs, from one QR code

Song requests + votes

Guests search any song, request it, and tap a heart to vote it up. One vote per phone.

Paid requests + line-jumps

You set the price per party. Paid requests jump to the top of your list.

Tips

Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, or card. Every tip shows up in your DJ booth.

Shout-outs

Guests send a message for the big screen. You OK each one first.

Invites, RSVP + QR tickets

Themed invites, guests pick up to 3 songs when they RSVP, and each guest gets a personal QR ticket.

Door check-in + VIP

Scan tickets at the door with a phone camera. Sell VIP upgrades, keep a VIP list, and take table requests.

Photo booth + big screen

An iPad photo booth with fun backgrounds. Guests send photos and 15-second videos, and you pick what shows on the big screen.

Bookings + Instagram codes

A booking form that lands in your customer book, plus “comment PARTY” codes that DM followers a VIP or free-entry pass.

Questions

Before you buy

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan a QR code and it opens in their phone's browser.

Does it come in Spanish?

Yes. Every page has an EN | ES button, and the sample contract comes in Spanish too.

Do I need my own website?

No. Every plan comes with one. Premium adds a custom website on your own web address.

How do contracts work?

On Pro and Premium, you send a link. Your client reads it, signs with their finger and pays the deposit right there. The date is locked once it's paid, and they get reminders before the balance is due.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Ask for the free demo and we'll make one with your name on it.

What do I need at the party?

Your phone. Add an iPad for the photo booth and a TV or projector for the big screen if you want.
