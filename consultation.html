How it works

Four steps, and we do the hard part

Step1

Answer a few questions

A short interview by message or a 15-minute call: your idea, city, money, credit, time and goal.

Step2

The team researches

Researchers dig into your market, competitors, costs, funding, credit, staff and permits, using official and expert sources first.

Step3

We try to sink it

A checker re-checks every number, and a Red Team looks for the ways your business could fail. Most plans skip this.

Step4

You get your plan

A straight verdict, your #1 focus for the next 90 days, and step-by-step actions with dates and costs.

Questions

Before you buy

Is this a person or AI?

It's an AI consulting team run with Claude, set up and checked by UAOK before it reaches you.

Is this legal, tax or financial advice?

No. It's research and a plan to help you decide. Before you sign a loan or a lease, take it to a CPA or a business attorney. The plan lists free local help.

Will you get me a loan or a grant?

No, we're not a lender. The plan shows which loans and grants fit your credit and cash, and how to apply. We never charge a fee to find you money.

What if the answer is No?

Then the plan says so, explains why, and suggests a nearby idea that could work instead. Better to know before you spend.

Is this for an idea or a business I already run?

Both. For a running business, the plan focuses on growth: more customers, better prices, funding and hiring.
