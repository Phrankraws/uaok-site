No. We can use a made-up spokesperson, a cartoon mascot, or no person at all.

Which apps can you post to?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn. Tell us which ones you use.

Can I change a post?

Yes. Ask for changes before you approve. Each post gets one free round of changes.
